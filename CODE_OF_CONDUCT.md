# Code of conduct

Be respectful in issues and discussions. Many people are fixing a family member's TV for the first time.

## Expected

- Be kind and specific (model, Android version, firmware build when reporting problems).
- Do not publish Wi-Fi names, IP addresses, serial numbers, account emails, or ADB keys in public posts or commits.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, slurs, or personal attacks.
- Instructions that uninstall system apps, require root, or intentionally brick a device.

Maintainers may close or lock threads that violate this code.
